# The Ballpark

**Local 22 retro pay estimator** for the 2025–2027 Act 111 Award.

Enter your rank/step and dates, and get a ballpark estimate of your retro check. Built to work on your phone.

## Use it

Open the live version here:

**https://awswisher.github.io/the-ballpark/**

Or download [index.html](index.html) and open it in any browser — it works offline.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves your phone — there's no server, no tracking, and no data collection.

## Disclaimer

Estimates only. This is not an official calculation — your actual retro pay is determined by the City and the Award. Always verify against your pay stubs.
