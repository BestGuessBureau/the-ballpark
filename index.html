Estimated take-home

Rough only — actual withholding depends on the City's payroll method, your W-4, and year-to-date wages. Retro is usually taxed as supplemental pay: many payrolls withhold federal at a flat 22% (the default below), so the check can look smaller than the gross number suggests. Over-withholding evens out when you file. Rates are editable.

Deduction rates
Federal withholding (supplemental flat rate) Social Security — Philadelphia uniformed members are typically not in Social Security. Enter 6.2 only if your stub shows an OASDI / Social Security line. Medicare Pennsylvania income tax Philadelphia wage tax — resident rate; non-residents enter 3.425 Pension contribution — retro base pay is usually pensionable. Enter the pension % from your pay stub; don't leave 0 unless your stub shows no pension line.

What this does not cover: partial or prorated checks, unpaid leave, retirements mid-period, and any pay line not entered in the OT log. Payroll may prorate boundary checks or treat holiday hours differently. City service time controls longevity.

Everything is gross pay before taxes unless labeled otherwise. Treat the result as an estimate, not a promise — payroll's number is the real one.
